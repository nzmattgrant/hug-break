Hug Break

Little reminders to hug the people you love.

A cosy iPhone app that nudges you to take a hug break, keeps track of your hug streak, and helps you send a little love when you can't be together.

Coming soon to the App Store

⏰ Gentle reminders

Pick set times, every few hours, or a surprise random time each day.

💌 Can't hug? Send love

Sweet messages for every mood, sent straight to Messages, WhatsApp, Telegram, Viber, LINE, KakaoTalk or WeChat.

🔥 Hug streaks

Tap "We hugged!" and watch your streak grow, day after day.

🔒 Totally private

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.

🌙 Light or dark

Match your phone, or pick the look you like.

🌐 8 languages

English, Deutsch, Español, Français, Italiano, 日本語, 한국어 and 中文.
