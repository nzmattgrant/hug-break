Common questions

My reminders don't arrive.

Check that notifications for Hug Break are on in your phone's Settings, and that the reminder is switched on with at least one day picked.

Why are my reminders only planned up to a certain date?

An iPhone holds at most 64 upcoming alerts per app. If your reminders need more than that, Hug Break plans the soonest ones and adds more each time you open the app.

My random-time reminders stopped.

Random times are planned a week ahead. Open the app at least once a week to keep them coming.
